**Figure 5. Separate animal-training experiments with matched additions and held-out human evaluation.** **a**, GSE325993 measured *POU5F1* CRISPR interference (CRISPRi) and non-targeting controls in two human and two cynomolgus induced pluripotent stem cell (iPSC) lines. The initial screen comprised 6,169 human and 3,563 macaque cells; 6,357 measured genes matched the State axis. H uses 256 cells from the non-held-out human line. Augmented groups add 256 human, M56 or M82 cells; A0 substitutes disjoint animal control targets. Selected models are evaluated on the held-out human line. Frozen models are then evaluated using 12,136 cells from the same two human lines in exp3. **b**, The lipopolysaccharide (LPS) test uses ten human CD14 monocyte donors (1,644 cells; Lawlor et al.). Two selected rabbit bone-marrow phagocyte sources supply 638 cells including A0 (Hagai et al.). The comparison uses 4,633 measured genes. Human and rabbit protocols differ: 24 h versus 6 h stimulation and 10 versus 100 ng/ml LPS. Human, rabbit and rabbit A0 additions match within each donor fold. Held-out human donors are scored in five folds. H is the human-only baseline; A0 retains the real source’s training-amplitude coefficient. Cell groups, assay wells and matrix tiles are schematic. Pred., predicted; Obs., observed. The studies are fitted separately.
