**Figure 6. Animal-training benefit is source- and target-dependent.** **a,b**, Response mean squared error (MSE) in the initial induced pluripotent stem cell (iPSC) screen (exp2) and frozen second-screen evaluation (exp3). Symbols identify the two human target lines, each averaged over three separately scored sampling/order seeds. Black segments show the equally weighted two-line mean. H denotes human-only fitting; +Human adds cells from the same training human line. M56 and M82 are separate macaque sources. A0 replaces perturbed targets with disjoint non-targeting control (NTC) targets while retaining the corresponding real-source amplitude coefficient. The 0.75H control rescales the H response without animal supervision. Panels a and b use different vertical ranges because absolute errors differ between screens. **c**, Paired additional-human minus M56 MSE for every exp3 seed in each target line. Positive values favor M56; the final segment is the equally weighted line mean. **d**, H minus augmented-model MSE in each of ten held-out human donors in the separate rabbit–human lipopolysaccharide (LPS) task. Positive values indicate improvement; black segments show donor-macro means. Every donor worsens with rabbit supervision and improves with additional human donors. Points denote repeated-model seeds in c and biological donors in d; overlapping training folds preclude treating all scores as independent replicates. Points and summaries are descriptive. The macaque analysis comprises two biological target lines.
