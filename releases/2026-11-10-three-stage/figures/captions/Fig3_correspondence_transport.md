**Figure 3. Correspondence-sensitive gains within a study transfer unevenly to another study.** **a**, The complete five-arm comparisons in two overlapping TG-GATEs cohorts: animal exposure matched (107 compounds, circles) and both species’ exposure matched (49 compounds, diamonds). Labels specify fitting/query correspondence. Small points show all 20 prespecified mismatches for each non-deterministic arm; larger symbols give medians. Deterministic arms are shown once. The dashed line is the no-animal baseline. **b**, Post-hoc response identification within the same outer-test fold and exact exposure stratum. Dashed segments indicate compound-averaged, query-specific chance. **c**, Frozen TG-GATEs rules applied to the three GSE330151 drugs on 7,307 shared genes. Each point is one drug/rule error divided by that drug’s zero-response error; points are horizontally offset for visibility. No external human outcomes entered fitting. **d**, Post-hoc scalar source-swap diagnostic. Each row is normalized to its matched-source error, using the same TG-GATEs-fitted scalar for every pairing. Off-diagonal substitutions also change animal dose and sometimes time. The cohorts in **a,b** overlap; mismatch runs reuse their biological observations. The three external drugs provide a retrospective cross-study stress test. Human-record sensitivity analyses and exposure ranges are reported in Supplementary Methods 1.
