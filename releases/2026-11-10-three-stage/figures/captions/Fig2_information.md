**Figure 2. Predictive information in measured rat responses.** a, Compound-level mean squared errors on 126 compounds and 678 conditions, with all 10,358 orthologues retained. Teal and rose points indicate lower and higher error after adding the rat measurement; both axes are logarithmic. b, Error reduction from the source residual beyond the chemical, exposure, projection-amplitude and root-mean-square (RMS) control; all 126 compounds are shown. c, Reference-coverage sensitivity. Points are three fixed nested subsample draws at budgets 16–64; the full-budget result is reused once. The line reports the relative difference of mean losses. These draws are repeated subsamples of the same biological observations. d, Terminal response identification for the joint-control and residual models among other compounds in the same outer-test time/nominal-dose stratum. The dashed line is query-specific chance averaged over compounds. Candidate absolute concentrations can differ. No feature or outcome-based panel selection is used.
